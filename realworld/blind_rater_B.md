- **25382, F rather than B:** the rotation setting was only partly honored, and the wrong output comes from rotated data being read as if it were unrotated.

- **21893, 23717, 28537:** the fix was named by a maintainer ("should/could be fixed by"). The race explanation comes from the maintainers' discussion on #25863 and #27311.
