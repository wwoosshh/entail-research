### Your current environment

<details>

- **vLLM:** v0.23.0
- **Model:** Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (BF16 and FP8)
- **GPU:** B300 GPU (single GPU, TP=1, DP=1).
- **dtype:** `torch.bfloat16`, `kv_cache_dtype=auto`.
- **Spec-decode:** `--speculative-config '{"method":"mtp","num_speculative_tokens":2}'` (also seen at depth 3). The MTP draft module is the model's own MTP head (unquantized).
- **Sampling (client):** `temperature=1.0`, `top_p=0.95`, no `top_k`, no repetition penalty, `max_tokens=20000`.

</details>


### 🐛 Describe the bug

### Expected

MTP spec-decode is a lossless acceleration: output distribution should match `speculative_config=None`. Right now, the output shows garbage output.

### Actual

On deep conversations, a fraction of turns collapse into a degenerate loop that runs to `max_tokens` and produces no usable output.

### Workaround

Disabling native MTP (`--speculative-config` removed) eliminates the degeneration. A repetition penalty / lower temperature might break the loops but was not validated.
