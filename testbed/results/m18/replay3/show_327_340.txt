BODY: ## Summary Per-head bit allocation based on attention entropy improves KV cache quantization quality by **+8% BLEU** and **+17% token match** at the same compression ratio (4x). This is relevant to the existing TurboQuant integration (#38171) and INT8 KV cache work (#33480) -- entropy-adaptive allocation enhances both approaches. ## The Math Optimal bit allocation per head: ``` b_h = b_avg + 0.25 * (H_avg - H_2(h)) ``` Where `H_2(h)` is the Renyi entropy of head h's attention distribution. Skipping quantization on just **3 heads out of 144** (GPT-2) provides more benefit than optimal bit redistribution across all other heads. These sink heads have near-zero entropy (H_2 < 0.4), meaning any quantization error passes through at full magnitude.
